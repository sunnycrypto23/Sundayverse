Sunday Michael. home log about contact study GST 112 Entry 002 · Aug 2026 Our GST 112 study session — and a mock CBT to go with it A few of us got together to go through GST 112 — Nigerian Peoples and Culture — ahead of the CBT. Instead of just re-reading notes back and forth, we split the syllabus into sections: the historical foundations of Nigerian arts and culture, the amalgamation and colonial period, ethnic and linguistic diversity, trade and self-reliance, post-independence challenges, law and social justice, and the norms and values section. Going topic by topic made it a lot easier to spot the parts nobody actually understood versus the parts we just hadn't looked at yet. Why we built a mock test out of it Reading through notes is one thing — recalling the answer under CBT conditions is a different skill entirely. So instead of stopping at notes, we turned the session into a proper practice CBT: questions pulled from every section, multiple choice, options shuffled on every attempt so nobody just memorizes "the answer is option A." You can run it section by section, or do the full mixed set in one go. It tracks your score per section so you can see exactly where you need to go back and read more. Try the GST 112 mock CBT → Testing yourself beats re-reading. If you can answer it cold, in a random order, with the options shuffled — you actually know it.
