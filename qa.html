Q&A — GST 112 Nigerian People and Culture

Below are sample questions from the summary. If you have a question, use my social links or email below.

Sample Questions

1. What are the three largest ethnic groups in Nigeria?

Hausa‑Fulani (North), Yoruba (Southwest), and Igbo (Southeast). There are over 250 other ethnic groups.

2. Define culture.

Culture is the total way of life of a people, including their language, beliefs, customs, arts, and social institutions.

3. List four components of culture.

Language, religion, cuisine, dress, music, dance, and social norms.

4. What is cultural diversity?

Cultural diversity refers to the existence of different cultural groups within a society, each with its own distinct practices and values.

5. Name two Nigerian festivals and their ethnic groups.

Argungu Fishing Festival (Hausa/Fulani) and Eyo Festival (Yoruba).
